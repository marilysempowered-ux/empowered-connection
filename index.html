How I work

My approach is somatic, holistic & trauma informed.

01

Somatic

Somatic comes from the Greek "sōmatikos" which means "of the body." A somatic approach acknowledges the upmost importance of a regulated nervous system to experience true fulfillment. It also recognizes that the body can store traumatic experiences & memories which unconsciously impact present behaviors even when it seems to go against what the conscious mind knows & desires. Many issues stem from a disconnection between the mind & body. As we reconnect with the wisdom & natural healing power of our bodies, beautiful releases can occur, freeing the system and allowing old patterns to shift with ease. It is truly empowering to slow down to listen, rebuild trust with our body & learn to regulate our nervous system. That is when magic unfolds.

02

Holistic

Holistic emphasizes the interconnectedness within the multidimensional aspects of our human experience. It recognizes that mindset and physical health is greatly impacted by the physical & social environment one lives in. When a challenge is showing up in one's life, it impacts all aspects of their life to some degree. As in holistic medicine, the focus is not on the symptom but on the whole system interactions & root cause.

03

Trauma-Informed

Trauma-informed because our deepest relational challenges of the present are typically connected to our most challenging experiences from earlier in life — and understanding that connection is often the first step towards creating a new narrative. A trauma-informed approach supports the system in releasing stuck energy in the body from a regulated nervous system, creating a greater sense of safety & security within.

About Intimacy

Many associate intimacy with sexuality. Of course sexuality is inherently intimate, yet intimacy is so much more. It is what allows an experience, a relationship to feel truly connective & fulfilling. Intimacy is a feeling of closeness and connection. It is when we feel seen & loved into the depth of our being.

It can happen during a sexual exchange or it can be solely emotional intimacy. Intimacy involves being open and vulnerable. That is what deepens connection and a sense of closeness which can even be experienced as a feeling of oneness at times. Tantric intimacy unites body, heart, mind, spirit and soul in the pleasures of living and loving. When truly embodied, it is enlivening and can be experienced everywhere, in all areas of our lives. It is so beautiful, powerful & can feel ecstatic.

My belief is that most humans crave for more intimacy without knowing how to nurture it outside of sexual context.

About Empowerment

Power is the ability to influence the course of events, to have impacts in our world. We all have this ability and are all powerful beyond measure.

The definition of empowering is 'to give power or authority to…' We can empower ourselves and also others! We can knowingly choose to use our power and join it to someone else's to support them in their endeavor — and we can also give our power away unconsciously sometimes (when we blame others for how we feel by example) which usually causes a feeling of helplessness in ourselves.

Empowerment is to consciously develop more awareness, skills, agency and intentionality around how we use our inherent power for more fulfilling relationships & life experiences.
